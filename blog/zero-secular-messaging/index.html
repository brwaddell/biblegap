Why Parents Can Trust BibleGap’s Theological Soundness: No Secular Messaging

BibleGap keeps kids’ Scripture learning free of secular messaging and ads, rooted in orthodox Christian theology parents can trust.

Walter Strickland·September 10, 2026

Safe digital content is hard to find

Finding safe, reliable digital content for children in today’s media landscape feels increasingly difficult. Modern children’s entertainment is frequently loaded with subtle ideological messaging, third-party advertisements, and diluted moral narratives. Parents wanting to pass down historic Christian truths often have to prescreen every book, video, and app their children touch.

Our promise: zero secular messaging

BibleGap operates under a strict promise: zero secular messaging, zero advertisements, and an uncompromising commitment to orthodox Christian theology. Our story framework adheres to historic biblical truths summarized in the Apostles’ and Nicene Creeds. We do not alter biblical accounts to fit cultural trends, nor do we dilute essential truths to make them politically palatable.

What kids actually learn

Every story points readers to Christ and grounds them in the doctrinal pillars of the Christian faith. Children learn about God as the sovereign Creator, the reality of human fallibility, the divinity and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ, and salvation by grace through faith.

By presenting Scripture as a unified narrative pointing directly to redemption in Christ, the app helps young minds build a robust, biblically grounded worldview.

Peace of mind for parents

Parents can hand BibleGap to their children with complete peace of mind, knowing every story summary, vocabulary breakdown, and interactive prompt has been vetted for theological accuracy. There are no surprise pop-up ads, no data-tracking sponsors, and no subtle worldview shifts.

It is pure, Christ-centered Scripture learning designed to build a firm foundation of faith that lasts.
