Faithful grandparenting

Passing the Baton of Faith: How Grandparents Can Gift Digital Discipleship

Faithful grandparenting means passing on a legacy of faith. A one-time BibleGap purchase lets grandchildren practice reading Scripture during visits and from far away.

BibleGap Educators·October 8, 2026

Grandparents hold a unique and honored place in the family unit. Grandparents feel the deep responsibility of passing down a legacy of faith to the next generation. Yet, finding meaningful ways to invest in a grandchild’s spiritual growth can be difficult—especially when competing against endless piles of plastic toys that are quickly forgotten or discarded.

Gifting BibleGap, a one-time purchase, gives grandchildren meaningful reading opportunities that strengthen faith and literacy during cherished visits. It offers grandparents a direct way to support their grandchildren’s spiritual development. Rather than giving a gift that lasts a few weeks, BibleGap equips children with literacy tools that build a lifelong habit of daily Scripture reading.

For long-distance grandparents, this gift creates a powerful spiritual bridge. As your grandchildren advance through reading levels and explore new narrative collections, you can follow along with their milestones and celebrate their progress during phone calls or visits. It shifts conversations from polite small talk to meaningful discussions about the Bible passages they read that morning.

Investing in a child's ability to read God's Word independently is one of the most enduring gifts a grandparent can offer. By providing the tools necessary to bridge early literacy with daily Bible reading, you help lay a foundation that will shape their worldview for decades to come, leaving a tangible legacy of faith that spans generations.
