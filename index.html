Inside the app

Home, library, practice, and a parent report you can trust

Narration highlighting

Words light up as the story is read to them

When narration plays, each word highlights in time with the audio—so young readers can see what they hear. Tap a word to seek; follow the golden path across the page.

This is follow-along support, not a quiz. It turns listening into reading practice before they ever touch the microphone.

Listen & Compare

Practice reading out loud. Parents see fluency grow.

After the page is ready, your child taps the mic and reads aloud. BibleGap uses Apple’s speech recognition to compare what they said with the story text—then saves an accuracy score. No permanent audio recording is kept.

Kids get a simple prompt to begin. Parents get a running fluency average and sparkline in the report—so progress feels visible without turning bedtime into a scoreboard in the child’s face.

English & Spanish

Dual-language support for the stories of the Bible

Practice reading and listening in English or Spanish with a single preference. Same story art, same levels—switch the language your child meets on the page.

For bilingual homes, Scripture can feel like it belongs at the kitchen table, not only in “school English.”
